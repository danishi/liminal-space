How to play

Just walk

Drifting

There is nothing to win. Explore. Every level is chosen at random, unless you choose where to start from the title screen.

You leave a level when its signal (top right) fades out, when you walk through a door that hums, or when you fall into a hole.

Going deeper

The more levels you pass through, and the further you wander from where you arrived, the stranger and more cluttered things get. Residents may talk to you. Other things only watch.

Touch and gamepad
